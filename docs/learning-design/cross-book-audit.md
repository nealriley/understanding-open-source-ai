# Cross-book evidence audit — 16 September 2026

The claim register is the shared authoring record. `supported` means the specified passage supports a bounded statement; it is not independent replication. `attributed` means the evidence establishes what the author says. `unresolved` entries are investigation records and must not be cited as settled facts or quiz keys.

## Consequential decisions

- Remove homepage headline estimates of adoption, lag and alleged extraction volume. They conceal incompatible denominators and attribution.
- Retire all original numerical widget series. SemiAnalysis’s inspected prose gives Llama 3.1 a composite of 86, not the widget’s 94.0. The widget also mismatches catch-up baselines. Replacing the series avoids manufacturing comparability across eras.
- Correct the glossary’s universal assertions about synthetic data, RL, costs, architecture, API access and model nationality. Distinguish mechanisms from empirical prevalence.
- Keep the Chapter 1 pilot’s four-part release assessment. Its source audit remains applicable; licensing statements are release-specific, not general legal advice.
- Retain Gurley, Zuckerberg, Catalini, Lambert, Xu, Toner, Saxe, SemiAnalysis and Ihle as named arguments. A strategic explanation is not evidence of a private motive; a proposal is not enacted policy.
- Retain research abstracts only for claims they actually support. Do not present abstract inspection as full-paper review. DeepSeek-R1 methods were also inspected in sections 1.1 and 2.1–2.3 of v1.
- Put unresolved incident accounts, market figures and extraction counts in CL-104. Their omission is not a finding that they are false.

## Propagation and sequence

Batch 1 removes the most prominent unsupported summaries and chart series and establishes this record. Batches 2–4 replace chapter arguments and answer keys alongside their shared summaries. Batch 5 reconciles timeline, bibliography, study paths and the complete register. Intermediate review branches are not publication-ready editions; merge in order after reviewing the complete stack.

## Limits

This is a bounded editorial review, not a systematic literature review or source replication. Missing publication dates are explicitly null, never inferred from a reading-list annotation. Historical cases use a review date, and no live feed is introduced. Learner effectiveness is untested.
