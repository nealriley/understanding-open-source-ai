## Chapter pattern

Entry check → specific outcomes → access / artefacts / permissions → worked assessment → independent assessment → feedback → short application checks → later transfer to the decision memo.

The study guide's `O-01` is a unit-level summary of these three chapter outcomes. Its other unit tasks are authored additions to existing chapters, with review status recorded in `curriculum-map.json`.

## Research-design basis

These decisions apply the earlier research pack; they are design interpretations, not newly established effects in this textbook.

- D01–D02: [CMU alignment](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/design/learningobjectives.html) and [learning principles](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/principles/learning.html) inform observable outcomes, prior knowledge, and corrective feedback.
- D03: [IES study guidance](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) informs revisiting and explanation; no optimal interval is prescribed.
- D04: [4C/ID](https://www.4cid.org/about/) motivates a complete assessment task, without claiming full implementation of that model.
- D05–D06: [OEN organisation](https://pressbooks.pub/authoropen/chapter/defining-content-structure/) and [elements](https://pressbooks.pub/authoropen/chapter/defining-element-structure/) motivate separate course sequencing and chapter structure.
- D07–D09: [BCcampus style sheets](https://opentextbc.ca/selfpublishguide/chapter/style-sheet/), [accessibility guidance](https://opentextbc.ca/accessibilitytoolkit/back-matter/appendix-checklist-for-accessibility-toolkit/), and [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/start-here/) inform consistency, semantic markup, and distinct reader/author resources.
- D10–D11: [OEN quality dimensions](https://manifold.open.umn.edu/projects/a-guide-to-open-textbook-essential-and-exemplary-features) inform review. A learner pilot remains a project workflow choice, not a completed result.

These pedagogical passages were reviewed in the preceding project phase; they were not newly reappraised in this repository pass.
