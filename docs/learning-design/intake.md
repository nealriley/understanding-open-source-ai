## Assumptions and boundaries

The audience comes from the book, not a new learner interview. Reading routes, fictional cases, task criteria, and timings are authored design choices. They remain revisable after learner observation.

The pilot develops release literacy; it does not certify legal compliance, deploy a model, or establish model safety. It teaches readers to inspect exact terms and distinguish missing evidence from confirmed absence.

## Source handling

Use a definition's issuing body for what that definition says, the actual versioned agreement for its terms, and a research paper for its reported method or release. An author's essay can establish the author's argument; it does not independently establish all facts or causal claims in the argument.

Record uninspected or inaccessible references as unresolved. Do not strengthen a claim while simplifying it. Work from bounded source sections with stable locators rather than repeatedly summarising the whole collection.
